# Captain Hook Costume: Option 3, Premium

**Approximate total: $350–600+ CAD**
**Approach:** Use theatre-quality pieces: either **rent** a professional costume or **buy** a real wool frock coat and build around it.
**Deadline:** Party is Oct 31. ⚠️ Made-to-order coats from Etsy or eBay often take 2–4 weeks and may not arrive in time. **Renting is the safest premium choice.** Call this week, because late October is peak season.

---
