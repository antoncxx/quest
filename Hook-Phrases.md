# Captain Hook: Lines for Each Guest

Lines to practise for each character. They use only what Hook would know: his own card plus the famous stories. Say them in a posh, theatrical British voice, and treat them as starting points to improvise from.

---

## 🎩 Mad Hatter (your enemy)
*Goal: build tension through the evening, then confront him.*
- **Early, cold:** "Hatter. Lovely party. Pity about the company… *you*."
- **Passing by:** "Enjoy your tea, Hatter. It may be your last cup."
- **The confrontation:** "You have something that belongs to me, and I'll have it back before this tea goes cold!"
- **Threat:** "I've fed better men than you to the crocodile."
- **When he talks nonsense:** "Riddles! Always riddles! Answer me plainly, you mad milliner. *Where is my map?*"
- **If he refuses:** "Bad form, Hatter. *Very* bad form."

## 🐇 White Rabbit (you suspect he knows about the map)
*Goal: get information out of him. Be friendly first, then menacing.*
- **Friendly:** "Ah, the royal messenger! A gentleman who carries so many *letters*… and so many *secrets*, I'd wager."
- **Pressure:** "You're always in such a hurry, rabbit. Running from something? Or *someone*?"
- **His pocket watch (your crocodile fear!):** "Is that… *ticking*?! Put that thing away! Are you in league with the crocodile?!"
- **Bribe:** "Tell me what you know about my map, and you'll have a share of the treasure. Cross me, and you'll be *very* late. Permanently."

## ♥️ Queen of Hearts (don't anger her)
*Goal: flatter her and win her over. Never contradict her.*
- **Greeting (bow deeply):** "Your Majesty. Captain James Hook, at your service. Wonderland has never looked so… *commanding*."
- **Flattery:** "At last, someone at this party who understands how to run a proper ship."
- **Recruiting her:** "Your Majesty, the Hatter is hiding something from me. Surely a ruler of your power can't allow thieves at her table?"
- **If she shouts "Off with their heads!":** "*Splendid* idea, Majesty. Might I suggest starting with the Hatter?"

## 👑 King of Hearts (nervous, avoids conflict)
*Goal: have fun intimidating him, and maybe find out what he knows.*
- "Your Majesty… you seem *nervous*. A guilty conscience, perhaps?"
- "A king who fears his own queen? Ha! On my ship, I give the orders."
- "Relax, Your Majesty. I'm not here for *your* treasure. Unless you've got some?"
- "Tell me, King, does the Hatter keep secrets for *you* as well? He seems to collect them."

## 👧 Alice (the curious investigator)
*Goal: win her over. She asks questions, and you want answers about the Hatter.*
- "Ah, a curious young lady. Curiosity can be dangerous… but useful. Help me, and I'll make it worth your while."
- "You're a friend of the Hatter's? Then perhaps you can tell me what he's *hiding*."
- **When she asks why you're here:** "Unfinished business, my dear. The Hatter has something of mine."
- "Curiouser and curiouser, eh? Well, *I'm* curious where my treasure map went."

## 🐱 Cheshire Cat (the gossip)
*Goal: trade gossip. The Cat knows things it shouldn't.*
- "A cat who knows everyone's secrets? Then you and I should be *friends*."
- "I'll trade you a secret for a secret, cat. What do you know about the Hatter and my map?"
- **When it appears suddenly:** "Blast it! Stop *doing* that, you grinning furball!"
- "Careful, cat. I've dealt with worse beasts than you. Much worse. With *teeth*."

## 🐺 Big Bad Wolf (fellow villain)
*Goal: villain solidarity. Two men with bad reputations.*
- "Ah, a fellow *villain*! At last, someone with a proper reputation."
- "They call you the Big Bad Wolf; they call me a scoundrel. Misunderstood, the pair of us."
- "Tell me, Wolf, have you ever been chased by a reptile with a clock in its belly? No? Then you don't know true suffering."
- "Help me deal with the Hatter, and I'll help you with your… *public image* problem."

## 🧣 Little Red Riding Hood (looks innocent, famous for surviving the Wolf)
*Goal: enjoy the contrast between the pirate and the "sweet" girl. Be suspicious of everyone.*
- "So you're the girl who survived the Big Bad Wolf? Impressive. Either very lucky… or very *clever*."
- "That sweet little smile doesn't fool me, my dear. I've sailed with pirates who smiled just like that."
- "A basket of goodies, is it? Anything *valuable* in there? A map, perhaps?"
- "If you ever tire of the forest, there's room on my crew for a girl with your… *talents*."

## Lines for any time
- "Bad form!" (when anyone is rude)
- "Tick… tock… does anyone else hear that?!" (looking around in panic)
- "Smee! …oh, right. No Smee."
- "Captain James Hook, terror of Neverland, scourge of the seven seas!"
- "Revenge is a dish best served at tea time." (your exit line)
