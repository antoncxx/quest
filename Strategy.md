# Captain Hook: Quest Strategy

**Mission (from your card):** Confront the Hatter and demand your treasure map.

**Sources:** your own card plus each guest's *public* information only (title, personality, costume, line and the original stories). Nothing here uses other guests' stories, connections, missions or secrets.

---

## 1. What "winning" looks like

| Level | Outcome |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Mission complete** | You confront the Hatter in front of witnesses and demand the map. |
| ⭐ **Strong result** | The Hatter admits he has it, or other guests side with you. |
| 🏆 **Total victory** | You walk away with the map (or a promise of it), *and* you stayed in character all night. |

Your actual mission is only the first row, so you can't fail as long as the confrontation happens. Everything above that is extra.

---

## 2. Who's who: the board from Hook's point of view

Every guest is rated by how useful they are to you, based only on their public personality and title.

| Guest | Public profile | Role for Hook | How to handle them |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎩 **Mad Hatter**, *The Eccentric Host* | Theatrical, mischievous, unpredictable | 🎯 **Target** | He's the host, so he wants the party to go smoothly. A public scene puts pressure on him. He also loves a show, so offer him one. |
| 🐇 **White Rabbit**, *The Nervous Royal Messenger* | Anxious, secretive, overly polite, obsessed with punctuality | 🔍 **Main information source.** Your card says you suspect he knows something about the map. | He's nervous and polite, so **pressure works**. Corner him (politely) and react to his pocket watch. |
| ♥️ **Queen of Hearts**, *The Temperamental Ruler* | Dramatic, commanding, impatient, used to getting her way | 👑 **Most powerful possible ally.** Your card says not to anger her. | Flatter her and never contradict her. If she backs you, her word outranks the Hatter's. |
| 👑 **King of Hearts**, *The Nervous Monarch* | Polite, cautious, easily flustered, afraid of confrontation | 🪜 **A way to reach the Queen** | Easy to intimidate, but **don't bully him in front of the Queen**. Win him over quietly, and he may speak well of you to her. |
| 🐱 **Cheshire Cat**, *The Mischievous Gossip* | Playful, mysterious, sarcastic, unpredictable | 📡 **Gossip broker** | Trade information with the Cat. Assume anything you say will spread, which can work in your favour. |
| 👧 **Alice**, *The Curious Investigator* | Curious, clever, always asking questions | 📣 **Amplifier** | She'll ask you questions anyway. Give her your story ("the Hatter has something of mine") and let her curiosity add pressure. |
| 🐺 **Big Bad Wolf**, *The Misunderstood Villain* | Charming, sarcastic, defensive, a little intimidating | 🤝 **Natural ally.** One villain to another. | Bond over your bad reputations. Bring him with you to the confrontation for backup. |
| 🧣 **Little Red**, *The Not-So-Innocent Girl* | Sweet-looking, charming, "secretly cunning" | ⚠️ **Wild card** | Her public profile literally says *cunning*. Be charming, but don't share anything you need kept quiet. |

**In short:** one target (the Hatter), one information source (the Rabbit), one source of authority (the Queen), two allies (the Wolf, plus the King once won over), two people who spread news (the Cat and Alice), and one wild card (Red).

## 3. Three strategies

### Strategy A: "The Royal Decree" ⭐ recommended
*Get the Queen's backing, then make your demand with her on your side.*

1. **Early:** greet the Queen first, before anyone else. Bow and flatter her: *"At last, a true ruler among us."*
2. **Early-mid:** befriend the King quietly. Never mock him in front of her.
3. **Middle:** tell the Queen your grievance as an insult to *her* authority: *"Majesty, a thief sits at your table. The Hatter holds what's mine. Surely you won't allow it in your presence?"*
4. **Confrontation:** demand the map from the Hatter **with the Queen nearby**. If she says "Off with his head!", you've won the room.

| ✅ Strengths | ⚠️ Risks |
|---|---|
| Uses the most powerful person at the party | The Queen is impatient and unpredictable, so she might turn on you |
| Fits your card ("would rather not anger her") | It takes time to win her over |
| Very theatrical, and everyone will remember it | If you anger her even once, the plan falls apart |

**If she turns on you:** back off immediately (*"Your Majesty is, as always, absolutely right"*) and switch to Strategy B.

---

### Strategy B: "The Interrogation"
*Collect evidence first, then confront the Hatter with facts so he can't wriggle out.*

1. **Early:** find the **White Rabbit**. Be friendly first, then press him: *"You hear everything, carrying all those letters. What do you know about my map?"* React with horror to his pocket watch to keep him off balance.
2. **Middle:** trade gossip with the **Cheshire Cat**: a secret for a secret. Pick up any rumour about the Hatter.
3. **Middle:** let **Alice** question you. Tell her your side so she starts investigating too.
4. **Confrontation:** go to the Hatter with what you've learned: *"The Rabbit tells me…"*, *"The Cat says…"*. Even half-truths work if you deliver them with confidence.

| ✅ Strengths | ⚠️ Risks |
|---|---|
| Lots of conversations, so you'll interact with almost everyone | The Rabbit may be too nervous to tell you anything useful |
| The confrontation lands harder with evidence behind it | The Cat may pass your secrets on |
| Works even if the Queen stays neutral | Takes the most time |

---

### Strategy C: "The Broadside"
*Confront the Hatter early and loudly, then keep up the pressure all night.*

1. **Arrival:** look for the Hatter straight away. *"Hatter! You have something that belongs to me."* Mission complete in the first 15 minutes.
2. **Rest of the night:** keep raising it. Glare from across the room, toast "absent treasure maps", and tell every guest your grievance.
3. **Use your ally:** bring the **Wolf** into it as backup (*"My associate and I would like a word"*).
4. **Final push:** later in the evening, make a second, bigger public demand with everyone watching.

| ✅ Strengths | ⚠️ Risks |
|---|---|
| Completes the mission immediately, so there's no risk of running out of time | Fewer surprises later |
| Hook is loud and dramatic, so this fits his personality | The Hatter has all night to prepare a response |
| Creates a running storyline that others can join | You might come across as one-note, so vary your approach |

---

## 4. Combined plan (best of all three)

| Phase | Time | Do this | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Arrival** | First 15 min | Make a grand entrance. Greet the Queen first. Give the Hatter a cold, polite warning without the full confrontation: *"Hatter. We'll talk later."* | A + C |
| **2. Information** | Early-middle | Press the Rabbit, trade with the Cat, let Alice question you. | B |
| **3. Alliances** | Middle | Win over the King and bond with the Wolf. Flatter the Queen again and tell her your grievance. | A |
| **4. Confrontation** | Middle-late, with most guests present | Demand the map publicly, with the Queen nearby, the Wolf beside you and your "evidence" ready. | A + B + C |
| **5. Ending** | Late | Win: gloat. Lose: a dramatic villain exit, *"Revenge is a dish best served at tea time."* | — |

**When to confront:** do it once you have *some* leverage (an ally or a rumour) and a crowd is watching. Don't leave it to the very end in case the evening runs out. Avoid interrupting another guest's big moment, since it's better for everyone's game to get your own spotlight.

## 5. If things go wrong

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The Hatter answers in riddles** | *"Riddles! Answer me plainly, you mad milliner!"* Then take offence dramatically, which is your strongest card. |
| **The Hatter denies everything** | Name your source (the Rabbit or the Cat), even if it's only a rumour. Then threaten: *"I've fed better men than you to the crocodile."* |
| **The Hatter makes a counter-accusation** | Act outraged: *"Bad form, sir! Bad form!"* Then turn it around: *"Why does the host fear a simple question?"* |
| **The Queen turns against you** | Back off right away with flattery, then switch to Strategy B or C. |
| **Nobody helps** | Go solo: a loud public demand still completes the mission. |
| **Someone offers you a deal** | Hook is a pirate, so negotiate. A share of the treasure is a fine reward for an ally. |
| **You learn something about someone else's secret** | Use it as a bargaining chip, not a weapon. Ruining another player's game isn't good form. |

---

## 6. Rules to remember

1. **Never anger the Queen.**
2. **Always bring up the map.** Every conversation is a chance to find a clue.
3. **React to ticking** (the Rabbit's watch, clocks, tapping spoons). It's an easy laugh and keeps you in character.
4. **Be villainous in character and kind out of character.** Back off if someone seems uncomfortable.
5. **The confrontation is the mission.** Make sure it happens, publicly.
